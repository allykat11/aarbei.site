About.

Creative for the outdoors.

I'm Alex Kappers, a multidisciplinary designer and visual artist exploring the intersection of visual identity, digital experiences and image-making.

A little about me

I work across branding, website design, digital content and visual storytelling. I enjoy working with businesses and individuals who have something meaningful to bring into the world.

I'm particularly drawn to the outdoors and the natural environment. I believe creativity can be used as a tool for connection, awareness and positive change, and hope my work can contribute to conservation efforts.
