BRAND IDENTITY

Sage Yoga

Branding · Digital · Environmental · 2021–2023

Sage Yoga is a holistic brand developed from the ground up for a Stellenbosch-based yoga studio. Created during my final year of university, the project evolved into a living business, spanning identity, space, digital presence, and community touchpoints.

The visual language was designed to feel grounded, soft, and contemporary, balancing calm neutrality with warmth and approachability.

The studio's owner, my mum, particularly liked lavender and sage together. These became the foundation of the brand's calming colour palette.
